- created_at: "YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MMZ (state the clock basis if known)"

  group of exact object_ids. Never title/filename guessing. Scope
  may be one Knowledge Object, multiple Knowledge Objects, or a
  Knowledge Object group (CASE / EVIDENCE / HYPOTHESIS / LOOP).>"

## Observed Structure

What currently exists? (observations only — this section records
what was seen: isolated Knowledge Objects, repeated relations,
missing provenance links, fragmented CASE/EVIDENCE connections,
unconnected HYPOTHESIS, unnoticed LOOP recurrences. It does NOT
claim problems automatically; an observation is an observation.)

## Proposed Organization Change

Suggested organization (described, never applied): e.g. add a
relation between two exact objects; group related Knowledge
Objects; identify a possible LOOP pattern; connect an EVIDENCE
object to its relevant CASE; identify a HYPOTHESIS that would
benefit from more EVIDENCE. Each suggestion names exact
object_ids and the concrete structural change a Human COULD make.

## Evidence

- Supporting objects (exact object_ids, what they declare)
- Sources consulted (attributed, with acquisition context)
- Observed relationships (declared edges actually seen)

Evidence stays evidence; Inference stays inference — label each
interpretive statement as inference.

## Reasoning

Why this organization is proposed. Include:
- alternatives considered (and why not chosen)
- uncertainty (what remains unknown)
- unresolved questions (for the Human reviewer)

## Expected Impact

What may become easier to UNDERSTAND after Human application —
e.g. clearer lineage, easier navigation, reduced fragmentation.
Do NOT claim correctness improvement, truth discovery, or optimal
organization. Organization serves understanding; it proves nothing.
